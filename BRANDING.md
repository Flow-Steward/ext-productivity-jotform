# Branding

`icon.png` is the Jotform mark (the blue, orange and yellow strokes with the navy corner) as
Jotform's own pages show it for home-screen shortcuts, used only to identify this integration
in Flow Steward.

## Source

- Product: Jotform, the online form builder of Jotform Inc. — <https://www.jotform.com/>.
- Official asset: the 180 × 180 touch icon the Jotform API documentation page declares,
  `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="https://cdn.jotfor.ms/assets/img/favicons/apple-touch-icon-180x180.png">`
  on <https://api.jotform.com/docs/>, downloaded 2026-11-20 (PNG, 180 × 180, 8-bit RGBA).
- SHA-256 of the source: `734b98bc4163acffe779a3924d6bb1c43850472ef42e01947ed0923c4fa6d269`.

The page also declares a 57 × 57 touch icon and a 144 × 144 favicon; the largest square
image was taken.

## What was done

- Nothing: `icon.png` is the downloaded file, byte for byte (it is already a square PNG of a
  size Flow Steward takes).
- SHA-256 of `icon.png`: `734b98bc4163acffe779a3924d6bb1c43850472ef42e01947ed0923c4fa6d269`.
